OSINT

Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) is the practice of collecting and analysing publicly available information to support investigations, threat assessments, and research. OSINT relies on lawful, ethical methods and focuses on sources such as websites, public records, social media, DNS/WHOIS, public code repositories, and archived pages.

Notes

Quick overview

These notes cover practical OSINT techniques, safe tools to use, and steps for documenting findings. Always follow legal & ethical guidelines — only collect publicly available information and never attempt unauthorized access.

Cyber Investigation vs OSINT Analyst

Cyber Investigator

  • Scope: Deep technical investigations (forensics, network logs, device analysis)
  • Access: May require internal/system access, logs, or warrants (legal authority)
  • Skills: Digital forensics, incident response, chain-of-custody, specialized tools
  • Outcome: Evidence collection for incident response, legal proceedings, remediation

OSINT Analyst

  • Scope: Publicly available information collection and analysis
  • Access: Uses open sources only (web, social media, public records)
  • Skills: Search strategies, source validation, data correlation, reporting
  • Outcome: Intelligence reports, lead generation, risk assessments, context for investigators

Key difference: Cyber investigators often require privileged access and technical forensic methods; OSINT analysts collect and interpret information from open/public sources to support investigations or intelligence work.

All techniques on this page are for defensive and educational purposes only. Do not use them for illegal activity. For legal guidance, refer to the IT Laws (India) PDF above.
