Practical Checklists

Quick reference checklists you can use during an investigation.

Phishing Detection Checklist

  • Inspect sender address and headers
  • Hover and verify destination URLs
  • Check domain reputation on VirusTotal
  • Look for impersonation signs & urgency language

OSINT Investigation Checklist

  • Record search queries & timestamps
  • Collect screenshots & URLs
  • Capture WHOIS & DNS records
  • Correlate usernames across platforms

Evidence Handling

  • Document chain of custody
  • Use read-only copies where possible
  • Keep a log of access and actions
  • Store originals securely
